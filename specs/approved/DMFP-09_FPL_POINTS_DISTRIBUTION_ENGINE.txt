PURPOSE
-------
This document defines the engine that converts coherent football scenarios into
complete Fantasy Premier League points distributions.

For every player and Gameweek it must produce:

- expected_points;
- median_points;
- points_variance;
- probability_zero_points;
- probability_5_plus;
- probability_10_plus;
- probability_15_plus;
- selected_percentiles;
- component_breakdown.

It must also retain the joint simulation structure required by the optimiser.

Components include:

- appearance points;
- goals;
- assists;
- clean sheets;
- goalkeeper saves;
- penalty saves;
- defensive contributions;
- goals-conceded deductions;
- missed penalties;
- yellow and red cards;
- own goals;
- Bonus Points System score;
- awarded bonus points.

The engine MUST NOT calculate expected FPL points by independently adding
unrelated component means.

It MUST transform each coherent simulated match path into one exact integer
FPL outcome and then summarize the distribution over many coherent paths.

GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
-------------------
FPL scoring is deterministic conditional on:

- the official season ruleset;
- player position;
- official minutes;
- on-pitch intervals;
- goal and assist classifications;
- match events;
- all relevant players' BPS actions;
- fixture and Gameweek membership.

The uncertainty belongs in the football scenarios, not in hidden scoring
heuristics.

Therefore:

    FOOTBALL SCENARIO
    + IMMUTABLE RULESET
    = EXACT SCENARIO-LEVEL FPL SCORE

and:

    DISTRIBUTION OF COHERENT SCENARIO SCORES
    = PLAYER/GAMEWEEK FPL POINTS DISTRIBUTION

======================================================================
0. EXECUTIVE MODEL DECISION
======================================================================

0.1 SELECTED ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------
The production engine is a pure, scenario-level scoring transform followed by a
weighted Monte Carlo distribution summarizer.

For every DMFP-08 scenario:

1. load exact active FPL rules;
2. calculate fixture-level raw scoring components for every player;
3. calculate full match BPS for every relevant player;
4. rank all players and award bonus jointly;
5. produce fixture-level integer FPL score and component vector;
6. validate conservation/rule invariants;
7. aggregate fixture scores into Gameweek totals using coherent multi-fixture
   scenarios;
8. summarize weighted player and joint distributions;
9. estimate Monte Carlo error separately from football-model uncertainty;
10. publish relational summaries and joint scenario artifacts.

0.2 BONUS IS A JOINT MATCH PROBLEM
----------------------------------
Bonus is not a player-isolated expected value.

For each simulated fixture:

- compute BPS for every participant;
- apply the same match context;
- identify the match-winning goal;
- apply pass-completion bands and all negative actions;
- rank the complete BPS vector;
- apply official competition-ranking tie rules;
- award integer 0, 1, 2 or 3 bonus points, with more than three players
  potentially receiving bonus when ties occur.

Expected bonus is then:

    E[bonus_i]
      =
      sum_s weight_s x awarded_bonus_i,s

The engine MUST NOT:

- predict bonus from one player's expected BPS alone;
- rank expected BPS values;
- add a fixed "bonus likelihood" independently;
- ignore teammates and opponents;
- award exactly three players in every scenario.

0.3 SCENARIO OUTPUT, NOT ONLY MARGINALS
---------------------------------------
The optimiser receives:

- player marginal PMFs;
- full player-by-scenario Gameweek points matrix;
- scenario weights;
- component matrices where required;
- covariance/correlation summaries;
- threshold probabilities;
- Monte Carlo error.

This preserves:

- teammates competing for goals/assists/bonus;
- goalkeeper/defender clean-sheet correlation;
- attacker versus opposing defender negative dependence;
- scorer–assister positive dependence;
- card/bonus negative dependence;
- Double Gameweek dependence;
- captaincy upside and downside.

0.4 MONTE CARLO POLICY
----------------------
Initial defaults:

Development:
10,000 effective scenarios per fixture.

Normal production:
50,000 effective scenarios.

Priority fixtures/players:
100,000 effective scenarios.

Close optimiser decision or chip/captaincy analysis:
adaptively increase to 250,000 or until precision gates pass.

The effective number of scenarios is based on weights:

    ESS = (sum w)^2 / sum(w^2)

A run does not pass simply because its raw row count is large.

0.5 PRECISION POLICY
--------------------
For priority players, proposed stopping targets:

- Monte Carlo SE of expected points <= 0.02;
- absolute SE of P(10+) <= 0.003;
- percentile/quantile uncertainty reported and stable under batch resampling;
- probability/mean change below configured tolerance over successive batches.

These control simulation noise only.
They do not imply the football model is accurate to 0.02 points.

0.6 GAMEWEEK AGGREGATION
------------------------
A Blank Gameweek:
all player Gameweek points are zero unless the player has another fixture
officially assigned to that Gameweek.

A Double/Multiple Gameweek:
sum fixture scores within the same coherent Gameweek scenario.

Do not independently convolve marginal fixture PMFs when:

- injury in fixture one can affect fixture two;
- suspension/card consequences can affect fixture two;
- rotation/readiness states are shared;
- manager/player latent effects are shared.

0.7 CAPTAINCY AND CHIPS
-----------------------
DMFP-09 outputs raw player Gameweek points.

It does NOT apply:

- captain;
- vice-captain fallback;
- Triple Captain;
- Bench Boost;
- automatic substitutions;
- transfer hits.

Those are manager-state transformations in DMFP-12/13, applied to the same
scenario matrix so dependence is preserved.

======================================================================
1. FORMAL DEFINITION
======================================================================

1.1 FIXTURE SCORE
-----------------
For player i, fixture f and coherent scenario s:

    X_i,f,s
      =
        APP_i,f,s
      + GOAL_i,f,s
      + AST_i,f,s
      + CS_i,f,s
      + SAVE_i,f,s
      + PEN_SAVE_i,f,s
      + DEFCON_i,f,s
      + GC_i,f,s
      + PEN_MISS_i,f,s
      + YC_i,f,s
      + RC_i,f,s
      + OG_i,f,s
      + BONUS_i,f,s

Each component is an integer under the active ruleset.

1.2 GAMEWEEK SCORE
------------------
For fixtures F_g officially assigned to Gameweek g:

    X_i,g,s
      =
      sum_{f in F_g} X_i,f,s

The scenario assembly must preserve shared uncertainty across fixtures.

1.3 WEIGHTED DISTRIBUTION
-------------------------
For scenario weights w_s normalized to sum one:

    P(X_i,g = x)
      =
      sum_s w_s 1[X_i,g,s = x]

Expected points:

    E[X_i,g] = sum_s w_s X_i,g,s

Variance:

    Var(X_i,g)
      =
      sum_s w_s (X_i,g,s - E[X_i,g])^2

1.4 MEDIAN AND QUANTILES
------------------------
Use the weighted discrete inverse CDF:

    Q(alpha)
      =
      min{x : F(x) >= alpha}

Record this convention.

1.5 THRESHOLD PROBABILITY
-------------------------
For threshold t:

    P(X_i,g >= t)
      =
      sum_s w_s 1[X_i,g,s >= t]

1.6 COMPONENT EXPECTATION
-------------------------
For component c:

    E[X_i,g,c]
      =
      sum_s w_s X_i,g,s,c

Scenario-level component sum must equal the total exactly.

1.7 COMPONENT COVARIANCE
------------------------
Components are dependent.

Example:

    Cov(GOAL_POINTS, BONUS) > 0

The total variance is:

    Var(sum_c X_c)
      =
      sum_c Var(X_c)
      + 2 sum_{c<d} Cov(X_c, X_d)

The engine SHOULD expose component covariance for diagnostics rather than imply
component variances add independently.

======================================================================
4. SCORING TRANSFORM ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

4.1 PURE FUNCTION
-----------------
The scoring function:

- performs no web/API call;
- reads no current global season;
- uses no fitted player model;
- uses no LLM;
- receives exact ruleset and scenario;
- returns exact integer components;
- is deterministic.

4.2 FIXTURE ORDER
-----------------
For each scenario:

1. validate fixture/event coherence;
2. derive official FPL minutes;
3. score appearance;
4. score goals;
5. classify and score FPL assists;
6. score clean sheets;
7. score saves;
8. score penalty saves;
9. score defensive contributions;
10. score goals conceded;
11. score missed penalties;
12. score cards;
13. score own goals;
14. calculate BPS for every participant;
15. allocate bonus jointly;
16. add total;
17. run scoring invariants.

4.3 PLAYER POSITION
-------------------
Use target-season FPL position from PlayerFPLSeason.

Do not use tactical role.

4.4 INTEGER ARITHMETIC
----------------------
All scenario-level FPL points and BPS are integers.

Summaries are floating-point/decimal weighted statistics.

4.5 RULE IDS
------------
Every component records applied rule IDs.

13. BONUS POINTS SYSTEM — DEDICATED JOINT SUBSYSTEM
======================================================================

13.1 WHY BONUS IS DIFFICULT
---------------------------
Bonus depends on:

- every participating player's actions;
- position;
- minutes;
- scoreline;
- match-winning goal;
- pass volume/completion;
- save location;
- errors;
- cards;
- defensive events;
- ties.

A player's bonus distribution can change when a teammate or opponent changes,
even if the player's own event distribution is unchanged.

13.2 BPS VERSUS BONUS
---------------------
BPS:
integer performance score.

Bonus:
FPL points awarded after ranking BPS.

They are separate outputs.

--------------------------------

13.4 MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE EVENTS
------------------------------
Penalty goal:

- apply penalty-goal BPS;
- do not also apply position nonpenalty-goal BPS.

Pass-completion bands:
apply one highest matching band only.

Appearance bands:
apply one matching band only.

Penalty save reference:
official explanation says penalty-save BPS and inside-box-save BPS can both
apply, giving 11 total BPS for that action under 2025/26.

13.5 GROUPED ACTIONS
--------------------
Use integer floor groups exactly:

    floor((clearances + blocks + interceptions) / 2) x configured BPS

    floor(recoveries / 3) x configured BPS

13.6 MATCH-WINNING GOAL
-----------------------
Use the active rule and event timeline to identify the decisive goal.

The goal-event timeline is required.
Do not approximate from scorer and final score alone when multiple goals exist.

13.7 CLEAN SHEET BPS
--------------------
Apply active position and minute eligibility.

13.8 GOALS CONCEDED BPS

- cards;
- own goals;
- errors;
- tackled count;
- offsides;
- goals conceded.

13.10 BPS COMPLETENESS MODES
----------------------------
FULL_EVENT:
All configured BPS actions are explicitly simulated.

HYBRID_AUXILIARY:
Core DMFP-08 events plus dedicated auxiliary BPS event models.

HYBRID_RESIDUAL:
Core explicit BPS plus a residual BPS distribution for omitted actions.

OFFICIAL_RECONCILIATION:
Historical official BPS is used as the outcome label.

Production target:
FULL_EVENT or HYBRID_AUXILIARY.

Initial fallback:
HYBRID_RESIDUAL with visible uncertainty.

======================================================================
14. BPS AUXILIARY AND RESIDUAL MODELS
======================================================================

14.1 WHY REQUIRED
-----------------
DMFP-08 may initially lack complete simulation of:

- passes;
- crosses;
- key passes;
- dribbles;
- fouls;
- offsides;
- errors;
- big chances;
- being tackled.

Ignoring them biases bonus probabilities.

14.2 PREFERRED AUXILIARY MODEL
------------------------------
Model missing event counts coherently using:

- player role;
- minutes;
- team possession;
- match dominance;
- score state;
- opponent;
- latent tempo;
- historical rates;
- team/player allocation.

14.3 PASS MODEL
---------------
Simulate:

- attempts;
- completions;
- completion percentage;
- minimum-attempt threshold.

Pass completion band must be calculated from integer attempts/completions.

14.4 NEGATIVE ACTION MODEL
--------------------------
Cards/shots already linked where possible.

Model:

- offsides;
- fouls;
- being tackled;
- errors;
- big-chance misses.

Use hierarchical count/rare-event distributions.

14.5 RESIDUAL DEFINITION
------------------------
Historical:

    BPS_residual
      =
      official_BPS
      - explicitly_reconstructed_BPS

Fit residual conditional on:

- role;
- minutes;
- score;
- possession;
- explicit events;
- team;
- player;
- manager/context.

14.6 RESIDUAL CORRELATION
-------------------------
Residuals cannot be independent by player.

Use shared:

- team possession/pass factor;
- match tempo;
- referee/discipline factor;
- player role effects.

14.7 DOUBLE COUNTING
--------------------
The residual target and production explicit event set MUST share the same
version.

When an event becomes explicitly modelled, remove it from residual training and
release a new BPS model version.

14.8 RESIDUAL INTEGER
---------------------
BPS is integer.
Residual simulation should yield integer BPS or be discretised under a declared
model.

14.9 CONFIDENCE
----------------
HYBRID_RESIDUAL lowers bonus confidence and widens FPL point intervals.

======================================================================
15. BONUS ALLOCATION AND TIES
======================================================================

15.1 STANDARD COMPETITION RANK
------------------------------
Sort players by BPS descending.

Group equal BPS.

Maintain competition rank:

    rank starts at 1

For a tie group of size g:

- every player receives the award for the group's starting rank;
- next rank += g.

Award mapping under reference rules:

- rank 1 -> 3;
- rank 2 -> 2;
- rank 3 -> 1;
- rank >=4 -> 0.

15.2 EXAMPLES
-------------
No tie:

- ranks 1/2/3 receive 3/2/1.

Two tied first:

- both rank 1 receive 3;
- next player rank 3 receives 1.

Three tied first:

- all receive 3;
- next rank is 4, no further bonus.

Tie second:

- first receives 3;
- tied rank-2 players receive 2;
- next rank after tie may be 4 or greater.

Tie third:

- first 3;
- second 2;
- all rank-3 tied players 1.

Two tied first and three tied at next BPS:

- two receive 3;
- next group starts at rank 3;
- all three receive 1.

15.3 MORE THAN THREE RECIPIENTS
-------------------------------
Supported by design.

15.4 NEGATIVE/ZERO BPS
----------------------
If the top three competition ranks have zero or negative BPS, the official rank
rule still controls unless target-season rules specify a threshold.

Do not invent a minimum BPS threshold.

15.5 PARTICIPANT LIST
---------------------
Players with BPS under active rules must be included, including substitutes who
appeared.

15.6 EXACT INTEGER TIES
-----------------------
BPS is integer, so tie comparison is exact.
No floating-point tolerance for final BPS.

======================================================================
16. BONUS DISTRIBUTION AND EDGE
======================================================================

16.1 SCENARIO DISTRIBUTION
--------------------------
For player i:

    P(B_i=b)
      =
      sum_s w_s 1[bonus_i,s=b]

for b in {0,1,2,3} under reference rules.

16.2 EXPECTED BONUS
-------------------
    E[B_i] = sum_b b P(B_i=b)

16.3 NONLINEARITY
-----------------
Generally:

    bonus(E[BPS vector])
    != E[bonus(BPS vector)]

Therefore ranking expected BPS is invalid.

16.4 PLAYER COMPETITION EFFECT
------------------------------
Examples:

- a high-BPS teammate lowers player's bonus probability;
- opposing goalkeeper save volume changes all ranks;
- a defender clean sheet creates clustered BPS competition;
- penalty goals can be less BPS-dominant than nonpenalty forward goals under
  reference rules;
- one late yellow can change multiple players' bonus.

16.5 BONUS CORRELATION
----------------------
Bonus points within a match are constrained and often negatively dependent
across players, but ties can create shared positive awards.

The joint scenario matrix captures this.

20. MONTE CARLO ENGINE
======================================================================

20.1 RANDOMNESS
---------------
Use approved counter-based/random-stream design.

Each scenario has deterministic substreams:

- lineup/minutes;
- score;
- goals/assists;
- shots/saves;
- penalties/cards;
- defensive events;
- BPS auxiliary;
- FPL assist ambiguity.

20.2 COMMON RANDOM NUMBERS
--------------------------
Use common random numbers when comparing:

- model versions;
- ruleset sensitivity;
- transfer alternatives;
- captain choices;
- chip policies.

This often reduces variance of differences.

However, research shows common random numbers can sometimes increase variance
when induced correlation is unfavourable.

Monitor variance of paired differences; allow disabling by module/comparison.

20.3 WEIGHTED SCENARIOS
-----------------------
If DMFP-08 uses importance/scenario weights:

- normalize weights;
- calculate ESS;
- use weighted moments/quantiles;
- monitor max weight and degeneracy.

20.4 RESAMPLING
----------------
For optimiser convenience, an equal-weight systematic/stratified resample MAY be
created.

Preserve original weighted artifact.
Report resampling error.
Do not erase rare tails unnecessarily.

20.5 BATCHING
-------------
Generate in batches, e.g. 10,000.

After each batch:

- update means;
- variances;
- threshold probabilities;
- PMF;
- MC error;
- stopping criteria.

20.6 VECTORISATION
------------------
Scoring transform should be vectorized where possible.

Bonus ranking may use batch matrix operations by fixture.

20.7 REPRODUCIBILITY
--------------------
Record:

- root seed;
- RNG algorithm/version;
- stream allocation;
- scenario count;
- weight;
- worker partition;
- code commit.

Worker order must not alter results.

======================================================================
21. MONTE CARLO ERROR
======================================================================

21.1 SEPARATE FROM FORECAST UNCERTAINTY
--------------------------------------
Football uncertainty:
the width/variance of the player points distribution.

Monte Carlo error:
uncertainty in the numerical estimate caused by finite simulation.

They must be displayed separately.

21.2 MEAN
---------
Unweighted independent approximation:

    MCSE(mean)
      =
      sample_sd / sqrt(N)

Weighted approximation uses ESS and weighted variance.

21.3 PROBABILITY
----------------
Unweighted:

    MCSE(p_hat)
      =
      sqrt(p_hat(1-p_hat)/N)

Weighted:
use ESS approximation plus batch/bootstrap diagnostic.

21.4 QUANTILES
----------------
Quantile MC error is not adequately represented by the mean formula.

Use:

- batch quantile variation;
- weighted bootstrap;
- order-statistic/binomial intervals where applicable.

21.5 DEPENDENT/MCMC INPUT
------------------------
If upstream posterior samples are autocorrelated:

- use effective sample size;
- batch means;
- chain diagnostics.

Do not count correlated draws as independent.

21.6 REQUIRED REPORT
--------------------
- raw N;
- ESS;
- mean MCSE;
- threshold-probability SE;
- quantile stability;
- maximum scenario weight;
- stopping result.

21.7 ADAPTIVE STOPPING
----------------------
Stop only when all priority metrics satisfy configured thresholds or resource
cap is reached.

If cap reached:
publish with warning.

Not initial requirement.

======================================================================
23. PMF ESTIMATION AND LOG-SCORE SAFETY
======================================================================

23.1 EMPIRICAL PMF
------------------
For reporting thresholds/quantiles, weighted empirical PMF is valid.

23.2 FINITE-SAMPLE ZERO
-----------------------
A rare possible score may receive zero simulated observations.

This does not prove true probability zero.

23.3 EVALUATION LOG SCORE
-------------------------
Do not calculate infinite log loss solely because finite Monte Carlo missed the
realised integer.

Options, predeclared:

- increase simulation;
- Rao–Blackwell/conditional probability calculation;
- model-based exact/mixture PMF;
- conservative Dirichlet/kernel smoothing fitted only for numerical evaluation.

The smoothing method must be versioned and used consistently.

23.4 STRUCTURAL ZERO
--------------------
A score is structural zero only if the model/rules make it impossible.

Example:
no fixture and no administrative points -> nonzero points impossible.

23.5 TAIL BIN
-------------
Do not combine all high points into one public PMF bin if the optimiser needs
15+/20+ detail.

Compressed display and underlying artifact can differ.
