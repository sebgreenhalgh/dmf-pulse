PURPOSE
-------
This document selects the external data-source architecture for DMF Pulse under
one binding economic constraint:

    THE CORE DATA-SOURCE SUBSCRIPTION COST MUST BE £0.

The document covers:

- official FPL rules, deadlines, game state and outcomes;
- current bookmaker odds;
- match-result, total-goals and related team markets;
- selected EPL player props where a free provider actually returns them;
- exchange research data;
- fixtures, results and competition schedules;
- official and observed lineups;
- injuries, suspensions and availability evidence;
- price, ownership and transfer-flow snapshots;
- historical match and odds research;
- open football event data;
- rate limits, quotas and source scheduling;
- identity mapping;
- timestamps, freshness and deadline integrity;
- licensing, database rights, storage, transformation and display;
- fallbacks, manual evidence and degraded operation;
- source acceptance tests and implementation contracts.

It does not attempt to create a directory of everything that can be accessed.
It selects the smallest defensible free stack and defines when a technically
available source is still prohibited from production use.

ZERO-COST DEFINITION
--------------------
For DMFP-04, "completely free" means:

- no paid API subscription;
- no paid historical-data package;
- no paid live exchange key;
- no compulsory betting deposit, turnover or wager;
- no paid scraping service, browser farm, proxy network or CAPTCHA service;
- no purchase required to unlock a source;
- no multiple-account strategy to multiply quotas;
- no geographic or access-control circumvention;
- no hidden assumption that Sebastian later upgrades to a paid tier.

The target recurring data-source spend is therefore:

    £0 PER MONTH
    £0 PER SEASON
    £0 ONE-OFF DATA PURCHASES

This does not mean:

- unlimited requests;
- complete player-prop coverage;
- guaranteed uptime;
- permission to store or publish everything that is technically visible;
- zero compute, electricity, storage or hosting cost;
- a service-level agreement;
- that public data have no copyright, database-right or contractual conditions.

Infrastructure cost is governed outside this document. The data-source design
must remain operable on free plans and existing/private infrastructure.

GOVERNING PRINCIPLES
--------------------
1. ZERO COST DOES NOT MEAN ZERO RIGHTS.
   Publicly accessible data may still prohibit systematic database creation,
   redistribution, model training, public display or commercial use.

2. A SOURCE IS NOT APPROVED MERELY BECAUSE AN HTTP REQUEST SUCCEEDS.
   Technical access, contractual permission and third-party data rights are
   separate gates.

3. MARKET-ANCHORED, NOT MARKET-COMPLETE.
   Free team markets anchor score distributions. Pulse's minutes, role and event
   models allocate team outcomes to players. Missing player markets remain
   explicitly missing.

4. PROSPECTIVE COLLECTION IS THE PRIMARY HISTORICAL STRATEGY.
   No complete, lawful, zero-cost historical EPL player-prop archive has been
   identified. Pulse must begin time-stamped collection immediately.

5. RIGHTS OVERRIDE COVERAGE.
   A technically rich source with unresolved rights is conditional or blocked.

6. OFFICIAL FPL IS AUTHORITATIVE BUT NOT AUTOMATICALLY LICENSED FOR DATABASE USE.
   Official rules and game output control FPL truth. The Premier League terms
   currently restrict database creation from Website/App material without prior
   written approval. Persistent automated ingestion therefore needs a specific
   rights decision.

7. NO MARKET IS DATA.
   NOT_OFFERED, NOT_YET_OPEN, STALE, RATE_LIMITED, SOURCE_DOWN and UNKNOWN are
   distinct from a numerical probability of zero.

8. ONE ACCOUNT, ONE PERMITTED PURPOSE.
   Quotas are managed, not evaded.

9. FAIL CLOSED FOR CRITICAL STATE; DEGRADE FOR OPTIONAL EVIDENCE.
   Unknown deadlines, manager state, rules or identity can block a decision.
   Missing player props or exchange data normally reduce confidence instead.

10. PUBLIC/COMMERCIAL DMF IS A NEW PURPOSE.
    The private V1 rights profile does not authorise a future dashboard,
    newsletter, API, subscription product or downloadable dataset.

IMPORTANT LEGAL POSITION
------------------------
This is an engineering and research specification, not legal advice.

Where public terms are ambiguous, the document intentionally records AMBER or
UNKNOWN rather than inventing permission. Sebastian or a nominated specialist
must review controlling terms before live activation. Written provider
clarification can supersede the public-term interpretation only when captured,
dated and linked in the Rights Profile.

RESEARCH METHOD
---------------
The rewrite follows DMFP-01 R3 provider research:

- official pricing and documentation were preferred;
- provider terms were inspected separately from marketing claims;
- access, automation, storage, transformation, training, display and
  redistribution were evaluated separately;
- free-plan limits were checked at the research cut-off;
- unsupported completeness claims were rejected;
- historical/live modes were separated;
- every provisional source receives proof-of-access and rights gates.

Public documentation does not prove:

- actual 2026/27 EPL coverage on Sebastian's account;
- actual predeadline player-prop availability;
- operator lists returned in the UK;
- source-to-API latency;
- exact settlement semantics for every bookmaker;
- stable free-plan quotas;
- stable undocumented FPL web interfaces;
- a court or regulator's interpretation of database rights;
- permission for future commercial products.

0.5 REJECTION RULE
------------------
A source is rejected or disabled when any of these holds:

- a payment becomes necessary;
- access requires betting activity;
- free quota is available only through multiple accounts;
- geography/account rules do not permit Sebastian's use;
- terms prohibit the planned private processing;
- required rights remain UNKNOWN at activation;
- provider IDs cannot be reconciled safely;
- timestamps cannot establish decision-time eligibility;
- market settlement is not interpretable;
- coverage fails the accepted role;
- no safe fallback exists for a critical dependency;
- the source attempts to force logos/photos/raw redistribution into the product;
- a terms change invalidates the Rights Profile.

2. SOURCE EVIDENCE AND APPROVAL STATES

2.1 REVIEW LEVEL
----------------
Every material provider/source decision is R3 under DMFP-01.

2.2 SOURCE STATES
-----------------
DISCOVERED:
A potentially useful source exists.

DOCUMENTED:
Official documentation states a feature or limit.

TERMS_REVIEWED:
Controlling public terms were captured and interpreted.

TECHNICALLY_OBSERVED:
A test account/request returned the expected payload.

BENCHMARKED:
Coverage, latency, timestamps, quota and mapping were measured prospectively.

RIGHTS_APPROVED:
The Rights Profile permits the intended private processing.

PRODUCTION_APPROVED:
Technical, temporal, rights and operational gates pass.

DEGRADED_ONLY:
Source may support a fallback but cannot be a primary dependency.

RESEARCH_ONLY:
Source can be used only in labelled research/reconstructed datasets.

BLOCKED:
A named issue prohibits use.

WITHDRAWN:
Previously usable but invalidated by terms, cost, coverage or security.

2.3 NO FEATURE BY MARKETING CLAIM
---------------------------------
A provider page saying "all endpoints" or "all markets" is not proof that:
- EPL returns the endpoint;
- every bookmaker offers it;
- a selected player prop is available before the FPL deadline;
- source timestamps are usable;
- a field can be retained;
- the quota can support Pulse's cadence.

Only prospective coverage reports can promote DOCUMENTED to BENCHMARKED.

2.4 NO IMPLIED RIGHTS
---------------------
Silence is not permission.

A field marked UNKNOWN for raw storage, training, backup, public display or
redistribution remains blocked for that action.

5. MARKET-COVERAGE POLICY
======================================================================

5.1 BASELINE TEAM MARKETS
-------------------------
Production baseline MUST work with 1X2 only.

Where available, totals/BTTS/correct score/team totals improve score-matrix
reconciliation.

No architecture decision may make complete player props a prerequisite.

5.2 CLEAN-SHEET PROBABILITY
---------------------------
Preferred evidence:
- opponent team total under 0.5;
- opponent-to-score No;
- direct team clean-sheet market.

Free-data fallback:
- derive P(opponent goals = 0) from the market-constrained score matrix using
  1X2, totals, BTTS and prior model evidence.

5.3 PLAYER GOALS
----------------
Use anytime scorer when:
- player identity is mapped;
- quote is predeadline;
- operator/settlement is known;
- nonstarter treatment is known or uncertainty is represented;
- market is not suspended.

Otherwise:
- use team-to-player allocation from DMFP-07/08;
- set market state explicitly;
- widen uncertainty.

5.4 ASSISTS, SHOTS AND SHOTS ON TARGET
--------------------------------------
The Odds API documents these for selected EPL US-bookmaker props.

Policy:
- opportunistic;
- candidate-fixture only;
- no claim of full starting-XI coverage;
- no missing-to-zero conversion;
- operator/line/settlement preserved;
- Pulse models remain mandatory.

5.5 GOALKEEPER SAVES
--------------------
No selected free source is assumed to provide complete EPL goalkeeper-save props.

Use:
- opponent shots-on-target model;
- goalkeeper minutes;
- coherent shot tree.

Any observed save prop is supplemental only.

5.6 ONE-SIDED PROPS
-------------------
One-sided player Yes/Over quotes are not complete books.

They retain:
- raw implied probability;
- margin uncertainty;
- source/operator;
- market state;
- optional calibration.

They are not forced to sum one across players.

5.7 MARKET OPENING
------------------
Player props can appear close to kickoff, often after the FPL deadline for later
fixtures.

Pulse MUST produce a complete baseline before props exist.

5.8 MARKET DISAGREEMENT
-----------------------
Do not delete a quote merely because it differs.

Exclude only for:
- identity error;
- wrong period/line;
- stale/suspended state;
- impossible price;
- settlement incompatibility;
- provider duplication;
- postcutoff receipt.

Statistical disagreement is retained as uncertainty.

20. QUOTA AND CADENCE
======================================================================

20.1 GENERAL
------------
Quota is a decision resource.

Every source adapter exposes:
- hard daily/monthly limit;
- per-minute limit;
- remaining;
- reset time;
- estimated call cost;
- reservation;
- priority;
- retry cost;
- last header observation.

20.2 PRIORITIES
---------------
P0:
- deadline/rules/manager legality;
- 1X2 market.

P1:
- fixture changes;
- candidate injury/availability;
- selected totals/team markets.

P2:
- player props;
- secondary context;
- weather;
- optional research.

20.3 THE ODDS API CADENCE
-------------------------
Recommended normal policy, subject to monthly budget:

More than 72h before deadline:
- event discovery free;
- one low-frequency h2h snapshot per material update/day.

72h–24h:
- one h2h snapshot per 12h;
- selected totals/BTTS once if available.

24h–6h:
- one h2h snapshot per 6h;
- targeted candidate-fixture prop discovery.

6h–cutoff:
- h2h at selected decision times, not blind minute polling;
- targeted prop calls only where action-sensitive;
- stop after cutoff.

Final:
- one reserved final snapshot before execution safety buffer.

This is a quota envelope, not an SLA. Actual schedule is optimised monthly.

20.4 API-FOOTBALL CADENCE
-------------------------
- coverage: preseason/season change, then weekly;
- fixtures/schedule: daily and after detected revision;
- injuries/sidelined: daily, more near deadline if quota allows;
- lineups: one call per relevant fixture after expected publication, for labels;
- events/stats: one postmatch/finality collection;
- odds: secondary targeted only;
- no 15-second live polling on free quota.

20.5 FOOTBALL-DATA.ORG CADENCE
------------------------------
- fixtures/tables: daily or after material revision;
- results: after matches/finality;
- no high-frequency deadline polling.

20.6 OFFICIAL FPL CADENCE
-------------------------
Only after rights approval.

DMFP-05 may define a conservative cadence, but it MUST:
- share/cache responses;
- avoid per-user duplication;
- avoid mass manager reads;
- remain inside the Rights Profile;
- stop/reduce on terms/schema concern.

20.7 RETRIES
------------
- no retry for quota exhaustion;
- exponential backoff for transient 5xx;
- honour Retry-After;
- no unbounded retry;
- reserve-aware;
- one logical run retains all attempts.
