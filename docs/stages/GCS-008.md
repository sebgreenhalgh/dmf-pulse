# GCS-008 — goal and clean-sheet distributions

## Purpose

GCS-008 converts one fixture’s accepted pre-deadline evidence into a coherent joint team-score distribution. The joint score matrix is the canonical artifact; expected goals, goal PMFs, goals conceded, 1X2, totals, clean sheets, BTTS, and scoreline rankings are all deterministic views of that matrix.

## Upstream boundaries

### Stage 6 market evidence

The production adapter accepts the frozen Stage-6 `MarketConsensus` or `MarketNormalisationResult` public contract for `FULL_TIME_1X2`. It preserves the Stage-6 result hash and refuses evidence with a later `as_of` or mapping cutoff than the Stage-8 information cutoff.

The internal constraint contract also supports result, totals, team totals, clean-sheet, BTTS, and correct-score rows. Those additional families are exercised with synthetic fixtures and do not introduce provider calls or parallel market identities.

### Stage 7 minutes evidence

Stage 7 remains frozen. GCS-008 consumes a compact, read-only identity projection of each accepted home and away `team-minutes-projection-v1` artifact:

- fixture and team UUIDs;
- source UTC cutoff;
- accepted Stage-7 model family;
- dataset, model-artifact, scenario-set, and result SHA-256 identities;
- fixed sample count.

The home and away identities must refer to the same fixture, distinct teams, and the same source cutoff. Their fixture/team identities must match the Stage-8 request, and the source cutoff must not exceed the Stage-8 cutoff.

The supplied Poisson rates are treated as upstream rates conditioned on this bound Stage-7 context. GCS-008 does not copy player minute PMFs, redesign lineup mathematics, or reinterpret Stage-7 persistence.

## Baseline model

### Prior

For home rate `lambda_H` and away rate `lambda_A`:

`P(H=h, A=a) = Poisson(h; lambda_H) * Poisson(a; lambda_A)`

Both rates and all intermediate probabilities use exact `Decimal` arithmetic. Binary floats, NaN, and Infinity are rejected at public boundaries.

### Adaptive support

Home and away support expands independently from the configured minimum until the combined omitted joint tail is below `1e-10`, subject to a hard maximum of 36 goals and a hard tail limit of `1e-8`. Required totals or correct-score support extends the minimum grid with a two-goal safety margin. Unsupported market lines fail closed rather than being silently truncated.

The retained matrix is normalized and the omitted pre-normalization tail remains explicit in the artifact diagnostics.

### Market projection

Each market event is an indicator row over the score grid. GCS-008 minimizes:

`KL(p || p0) + 1/2 Σ w_k ((A_k p - m_k) / sigma_k)^2`

where `p0` is the prior, `m_k` is the normalized market target, `sigma_k` is market uncertainty with a configured floor, and `w_k` is capped within market family. The deterministic solver uses the convex dual, Decimal Newton steps, and backtracking line search.

If projection does not converge and policy permits fallback, the prior is emitted with visible degraded status and a typed solver error. Otherwise the run fails closed.

## Public artifact

`joint-score-distribution-v1` includes:

- fixture, team, cutoff, policy, prior, input, and result identities;
- the complete immutable Stage-7 identity context and context hash;
- the Stage-6 source hash when market consensus is used;
- exact 12-place score probabilities and PMFs;
- exact 6-place expected-goal measures;
- clean sheets, BTTS, 1X2, totals, and top scorelines;
- target, projected, residual, standardized residual, uncertainty, weight, family, and confidence for every market row;
- convergence, KL, RMSE, maximum residual, constraint count, and tail treatment;
- confidence grade and explicit reasons.

The model validator independently recomputes matrix simplexes, marginals, expected goals, clean sheets, BTTS, 1X2, totals, market residual counts, Stage-7 context identity, and the result SHA-256.

## Persistence and replay

Persistence is explicit and file-based. A distribution is written atomically to:

`<artifact-root>/football-events/score-distributions/fixture=<uuid>/as_of=<utc>/input=<sha256>/score-distribution.json`

Replaying the same semantic input returns byte-identical content. Different bytes at the same content-addressed path raise `ARTIFACT_IDENTITY_CONFLICT`. There is no mutable alias and no GCS-008 database migration.

## CLI

```text
dmf events score-distribution --fixture <request.json> [--artifact-root <dir>] --output json
dmf events explain-market-fit --fixture <request.json> --output json
dmf events evaluate --distribution <artifact.json> --home-goals <n> --away-goals <n> --output json
dmf events validate --distribution <artifact.json> --output json
```

All commands are offline and deterministic. Postponed, cancelled, and abandoned fixtures return blocked results without plausible score distributions.

## Evaluation

Finalized outcomes can be evaluated without mutating the frozen forecast using:

- exact-score logarithmic loss;
- 1X2 Brier score;
- home and away clean-sheet Brier scores;
- home and away goal-count ranked probability scores.

## Assurance

The implementation supplies:

- semantic unit tests for Poisson, adaptive tails, design rows, soft KL, fallbacks, and every derived identity;
- Hypothesis properties for simplexes, clean-sheet/expectation identities, complementarity, support stability, and invalid probabilities;
- Stage-6/Stage-7 contract tests;
- a reviewed golden fixture;
- integration and CLI tests;
- adversarial cutoff, invalid Decimal, impossible market, hash mutation, artifact conflict, and out-of-support tests;
- a fail-closed scope validator that excludes Stage-7, migrations, dependency locks, and unrelated modules;
- critical-module branch-coverage gates;
- packaged-resource and installed-wheel verification.

Generated evidence is not self-authenticating. Acceptance requires clean-checkout command results, CI identities, independent review, and human approval.

## Non-goals

GCS-008 does not implement player scorers or assists, shots/saves/cards, penalties, own goals, defensive events, event timing, full timelines, production bivariate Poisson, Dixon–Coles, or count-model challenger promotion.
