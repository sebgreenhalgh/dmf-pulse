# Executive assessment: DMF-MODEL-QUALITY-001

The current model's real predictive accuracy is **not established by the
permitted evidence available to this audit**. Genuine current inputs and a
successful legal optimization are demonstrated engineering behavior. There are
no matched finalized outcomes and frozen benchmark cohorts here from which to
claim MAE/RMSE, probabilistic skill, calibration or statistically better decisions.
No historical forecast was reconstructed using post-match information.

The most significant weaknesses are stale player-allocation evidence, pooled
auxiliary events, synthetic-fitted global minutes priors, current-club assignment
of historical GW aggregates, weak league-level score support where markets are
incomplete, permissive ordinary Monte Carlo gates, and missing joint decision
attribution in historical retained outputs. Grade E does not mean every player
has no individual evidence: historical individual channels coexist with pooled
components and explicit current fallback. It is not a measured confidence interval.

Owned counterexamples also expose two pre-existing contract risks: RPS changes
with zero-mass support representation unless the target grid is fixed, and the
Stage-11 tactical contract can reject a valid rare-tail PMF because its mean
exceeds p90. Resolve these through bounded metric/optimizer contract review
before comparative skill claims and tail-sensitive expansion. Accepted engine
and evaluation mathematics remain unchanged in this research slice.

The authenticated GW10 numerical/identity audit and exact arithmetic are kept in
the local ignored `review_pack/private-audit/GW10-AUDIT.md`. The public repository
does not receive owner forecast rows or a derived export. The retained marginal
means cannot uniquely decompose the team total or explain the historical
Palmer/Haaland captain-only comparison. Those terms remain unavailable rather
than being invented to make the presentation appear complete.

The first implemented improvement is an offline `dmf analysis quality` command,
with missing-versus-zero classification, numerical/identity diagnostics, Monte
Carlo context and explicitly limited team accounting. Three additive SQL views
expose retained player/fixture/GW point PMFs, minutes PMFs, component contributions,
bonus and sampling diagnostics using existing typed evidence. New writes and
copy migrations reject ambiguous identifiers; corrupt historical digests refuse
migration. Original snapshot JSON, v1 rows, publication hashes and numerical
engines remain unchanged. This improves auditability and access, **not a measured
forecast-accuracy result**. Test, runtime, wheel and review evidence is under
`evidence/tickets/DMF-MODEL-QUALITY-001`.

Existing three-GW Stage-11 execution and the real-player prospective custody
service are the first integration candidates. Both already exist. They need
qualified current inputs and protocol bindings, not replacement frameworks.
Stage-14 chip mathematics also exists, but real WC/FH/BB/TC recommendations need
legal candidate routes, actual chip inventory and horizon inputs, and common
exercise-versus-normal/hold continuation comparisons. The WC evaluator selects
the best supplied route; this is not proof of a globally optimal 15-player squad.
The current rolling adapter's independent-GW/no-information-revelation assumption
must be visible. Reliable 5/8-GW projections require validated transitions and
uncertainty, not repeated one-GW forecasts.

The next priorities are bounded mathematical contract review, membership-safe
historical participation, qualified
predeadline freeze/final-label/benchmark custody, existing captain-pair evidence
capture, minutes calibration and isolated existing player/team challengers.
Use paired chronological comparisons and GW-cluster uncertainty before promotion.
