## Current controlling evidence

Official FPL 2026/27 terms clause 28(d) prohibits automated extraction; clauses
27–29 govern credentials and Game information. The existing owner risk exception
is not Premier League consent or a licence. Official target-season guidance sets
the normal points lock to 09:00 UK time the day after the final GW match, with
extraordinary corrections still possible. The offline adapter pins this season
and policy explicitly, preserves the original lock and later correction
finalization/evidence, and requires complete identity/component reconciliation.
[Official terms](https://fantasy.premierleague.com/help/terms),
[official 2026/27 changes](https://fantasy.premierleague.com/help/new).

## Owner decision alternatives

**A — existing scope:** retain the bounded transient own-team workflow, with no
new storage/training permission. First resolve the separately applicable market
execution approval; normal dashboard Refresh remains blocked until then.

**B — expanded experiment, not executed:** propose bootstrap/fixtures plus one
GW-wide result and one separately qualified status observation for each selected
GW. Declare exact seasons/GWs, maximum calls, manual triggering, own-entry scope
where necessary, secure server-side credential references, correction checks and
deletion rules. Avoid player-detail loops initially. This needs a fresh explicit
owner decision and appropriate legal/source-rights review; risk acceptance does
not override the official automated-extraction restriction or supply a licence.

**C — independently licensed retained evidence, not executed:** request a narrow
EPL historical/current player-event quote and explicit training/retention terms.
Keep it separate from private FPL facts. Obtain independently qualifying final
FPL labels as a separate requirement; vendor events cannot silently fill missing
bonus/defensive/FPL-specific outcomes. Pin source/profile/version hashes and a
new prospective cohort before results, then evaluate through this ledger/UI.

Recommended combination: A for eligible transient private decisions, C for
retained minutes/event research, and independently qualified final-point labels
for real evaluation. B is a conditional owner/legal decision, not an automatic
engineering follow-on. No new parameters or model promotion follows from any
synthetic demonstration.

## Acquisition and quality controls

Prefer bulk catalogue/fixture/GW payloads and one canonical season/player/fixture
map. Retain corrections and exact vintages only where rights allow; deduplicate
by immutable content identity without turning private data into a cache. Reuse
the existing request pacing/budgets and sequential provider boundary. Do not
multiply provider calls from browser retries, hide quota failures or rotate
accounts/proxies. Report expected/observed/missing/duplicate players, schema and
component drift, source/usable times, and failed joins. No missing label becomes
zero. A known blank fixture set is validated explicitly; double fixtures must
reconcile individually and in aggregate.

The implemented registry currently qualifies owned synthetic assets only.
Real private raw/derived writes are denied before connection/storage; PostgreSQL
also checks source qualification, forecast identity, score-pair completeness,
label finality/correction links and immutability. Source/version qualification
remains a deliberate decision, rather than an arbitrary caller-supplied ALLOW.
