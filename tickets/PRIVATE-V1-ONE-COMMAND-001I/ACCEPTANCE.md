# PRIVATE-V1-ONE-COMMAND-001I engineering acceptance

The transient current hierarchy retains every positive official-FPL player/team/order row and
covers the complete current team catalogue with exactly one canonical status record per team:
`USABLE_UNIQUE_ORDER`, `AMBIGUOUS_DUPLICATE_ORDER`, or `NO_PUBLISHED_ORDER`. Unique sparse orders
such as `1,3` and `2,3,4` remain exact and usable. A duplicate order makes only its team ambiguous;
no identity, name, share, price, ownership or stable-order tie-break is permitted. Rows, statuses,
bootstrap payload SHA, observation/cutoff times and schema are semantic-hash inputs.

Only usable teams contribute their exact provider ranks to Stage 9. Ambiguous and unavailable
teams contribute no fabricated hierarchy and continue through the accepted current hierarchy,
positive historical penalty-share, and private no-retention positive goal-share proxy chain from
001H. Derived warnings `CURRENT_FPL_PENALTY_HIERARCHY_AMBIGUOUS` and
`CURRENT_FPL_PENALTY_HIERARCHY_UNAVAILABLE` reach private decision warnings and the human report.

The interactive `dmf pulse` command emits default-on progress to STDERR while the unchanged final
report remains on STDOUT. It times FPL and odds acquisition, identities, Stage 7, score priors,
execution sealing, every Stage-9 fixture, total fixture projection, Gameweek assembly, Stage-11
request preparation, exact optimisation, captain verification, paired comparison and total run.
Stage-9 fixture work, Gameweek assembly and exact optimisation emit truthful 30-second heartbeats;
the optimiser emits one non-cancelling long-run warning after five minutes. No percentage is
invented. `--no-progress` suppresses all observability output. Ctrl+C stops heartbeat activity and
does not create a partial private recommendation.

Acceptance requires sparse/duplicate/no-order, isolated-team, raw-evidence hash, deterministic
seal, Stage-9 filtering, 001H fallback and final-warning tests; ordered STDERR, nonnegative timing,
N/total fixture, heartbeat, no-percentage, suppression, unchanged-STDOUT, disclosure and interrupt
tests; affected suites; branch coverage; Ruff; strict mypy; frozen sync; build and clean-wheel
verification; repository and secret validation; and exact-final-SHA CI. The literal progress-on
live command runs only when both existing credentials and a runtime entry ID are present.
