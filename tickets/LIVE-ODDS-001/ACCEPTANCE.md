# LIVE-ODDS-001 acceptance contract

Engineering completion for independent review requires all of the following:

1. The branch descends exactly from the pinned main parent; no readiness merge, rebase, or bulk
   copy occurred.
2. The production default is one explicit stdlib `http.client` HTTPS transport. Certificate
   verification, the approved host/path, redirect refusal, connect/read/total budgets, bounded
   reads, safe headers, typed failures, bounded retry, and quota evidence are directly tested.
3. Runtime credential resolution is lazy and secret-safe. No key enters a representation,
   sanitized target, fingerprint, exception, log, model/dataclass serialization, fixture, or
   evidence artifact.
4. The request is exactly `soccer_epl`, `uk`, `h2h,totals`, decimal, ISO, with approved optional
   commence filters and the governed request cost.
5. A safely isolatable additive market family creates deterministic
   `ADDITIVE_UNSUPPORTED_MARKET:<key>` warning/drift evidence, is excluded from accepted current
   markets, and does not block otherwise valid H2H/totals material.
6. Missing, duplicate, incomplete, malformed, line-bearing, temporally invalid, or secret-like
   mandatory H2H material remains blocked. Totals retain inherited optional/degraded semantics.
7. Raw body hashes distinguish source payloads; the current accepted-market semantic hash is
   invariant to excluded additive markets under identical temporal/provenance context.
8. Rights remain conservative: required private analytical capabilities must allow; unknown raw
   storage, backup, and model training remain effective denials; no raw payload is retained.
9. Focused ticket tests and affected inherited tests pass; new critical branches have direct
   branch coverage; Ruff, strict mypy, repository validation, secret scan, build, and clean-wheel
   smoke pass or are truthfully recorded as environment/resource blocked.
10. Evidence is bound to the final HEAD. P0, P1, and material P2 self-review findings are closed;
    independent review and human acceptance remain pending.
