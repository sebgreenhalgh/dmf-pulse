# Player evidence chronological holdout protocol (inactive proposal)

This protocol prepares a future comparison. No model was fitted, selected,
promoted or activated; no real historical files or provider JSON were acquired.
Synthetic CP4 results prove engineering semantics only. No real predictive gain
is established. A concrete source/period/population/model manifest must be frozen
and accepted before any evaluation under this protocol.

## Models and estimands

Compare three predeclared candidates on exactly paired eligible targets:

1. The accepted Stage-7 minutes model with its existing current evidence and the
   current historical Grade-E allocation prior for downstream points. Preserve
   exact parent numerical/model/rules identities.
2. A position/role-only fallback, separately specified and versioned before use.
   Its priors cannot be invented or borrowed from final holdout outcomes.
3. An evidence-supported challenger with qualified starts/minutes history and
   preregistered window/shrinkage/feature policy. Separate source improvements from
   parameter improvements through an explicit ablation if both are changed.

Grade-E is a player allocation prior, not an already trained alternative minutes
model. Minutes evidence does not itself fix goals/assists shares, bonus, defensive
contributions or clean-sheet event exposure. Downstream points require all those
accepted components and qualified labels; their metrics are secondary to the
participation estimands.

## Population and information set

The manifest must name exact season(s), competition, canonical player population,
fixture assignment and decision origins. Include the full eligible mapped
population under a frozen policy, including zeros and explicit sparse/unavailable
states; do not select players after seeing outcomes. Predeclare excluded
competitions, unsupported minute conventions, abandoned fixtures, youth/debut
players, transfers, missing roster/role/minute labels and source attrition.

Use fixture-level START/BENCH/OUT, minutes and 60+ labels with exact historical
team membership and provider minute conventions. Missing lineup rows are not OUT.
Provider matchweek is not FPL GW; blank/double Gameweeks are handled by explicit
fixture joins. A player changing clubs retains canonical identity while historical
fixture team remains unchanged. Final FPL points are a separate source/semantic
qualification.

Every input, derived feature, mapping, prior, model and rules version must carry
its own rights qualification and usable cutoff. Current private FPL profiles deny
retained/training data; an allowed outcome source does not confer forecast-input
retention rights. Research source storage, transformed datasets, backups, derived
model use and post-termination obligations require their own decision. An owner
can approve project scope and expenditure, but cannot grant third-party rights.

## Chronology and leakage controls

Select earlier contiguous training, later validation and untouched final test
blocks by fixture date, after data availability is established. The exact block
boundaries and all model/window/hyperparameter alternatives must be frozen before
test labels are inspected. No random player-row split; simultaneous fixtures and
future team/manager/roster information must remain temporally coherent.

Use observation/mapping/label `usable_at`, not just kickoff or provider generation
time. Select the vintage eligible at each origin. Late corrections never silently
repair prior feature information. Datasets without trustworthy historical usable
times are RECONSTRUCTED and reported separately from LIVE_OBSERVED forecasts; the
old release date cannot be relabelled as availability at historical kickoffs.

Tune on training/validation only, then lock candidate identities. Use the final
test once for the predeclared comparison. Preserve all attempted candidates and
missingness/coverage results; do not choose a preferred challenger or holdout based
on final error. New hypotheses require a fresh future test block.

## Frozen metrics and uncertainty

Reuse existing Stage-12 kernels where they implement the required estimand; do
not introduce a duplicate points evaluator. The final manifest must bind exact
metric definitions/versions and target units:

- START/BENCH/OUT multiclass Brier and admissible log loss; per-state calibration.
- Unconditional minute MAE, RMSE and signed bias; full minute-distribution RPS and
  admissible log loss.
- 60+ Brier and calibration, conditioned on exact qualified minute semantics.
- Downstream FPL points mean/proper probabilistic error and component diagnostics
  only where all dependencies and actual labels qualify.
- Paired target differences, coverage, attrition, missingness and denominator for
  every aggregate; position/team/new-player/transfer slices fixed in advance.

Count distributions from CP4 are descriptive support, not challenger predictive
PMFs. The model must supply a coherent unconditional START/BENCH/OUT mixture and
conditional minute distributions consistent with the accepted eleven-player team
constraints. Sample uncertainty should respect fixture/GW clustering: preregister
the block method/seed/replication count before evaluating. Always report effective
block count, sample support and interval limitations. Tiny samples or an
unavailable benchmark produce explicit insufficient/unavailable status rather
than false success. No numeric promotion threshold is invented by this proposal.

## Outcome custody and acceptance

Freeze the exact forecasts, cutoff manifests, cohort IDs and experiment identity
before their future deadline, but only after every retained dependency qualifies.
Authenticate later finalized labels and preserve original plus append-only
corrections. Join exact season/player/fixture/GW identities, reject duplicates and
partial finality, and account for all frozen rows. Use the existing operational
player-lab custody/evaluation runner rather than writing a parallel ledger.

Technical acceptance of the evidence slice, source acquisition approval, retained
research permission, expenditure, first real prospective-cohort authorization,
model promotion and production activation are separate decisions. Real eligible
cohort size for this slice is zero. Until those requirements are met, maintain
`NO RIGHTS-QUALIFIED REAL PROSPECTIVE COHORT`.
