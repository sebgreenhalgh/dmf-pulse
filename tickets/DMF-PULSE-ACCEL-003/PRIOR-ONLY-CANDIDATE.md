# Inactive governed prior-only fixture candidate

`project_prior_only_candidate` is a complete bounded offline fixture adapter,
classified `REPOSITORY_OWNED_SYNTHETIC`, status `INACTIVE_OFFLINE_ONLY`, mode
`GOVERNED_PRIOR_ONLY`. Its report explicitly says
`NOT_CONNECTED_TO_PRIVATE_WORKFLOW`. It is not exposed as a Refresh alternative.

The adapter requires exact official fixture lookup identities, distinct official
teams, canonical fixture/Stage-7/prior identity agreement, a single cutoff, a
declared one- or three-GW fixture assignment and the existing Stage-8 policy
hash. It checks unique complete fixture coverage, including explicitly blank
GWs; rejects missing or ambiguous fixtures, post-cutoff identities, cancelled or
already started fixtures, policy changes, market evidence and active labels.
Its inputs carry sealed owned prior requests; no invented market observation,
`NO_EVENT` or `H2H_UNAVAILABLE` wrapper is accepted or constructed.

The unchanged `ScoreDistributionService` handles each fixture with no market
constraints. Existing Stage-8 mathematics returns the supplied statistical prior
grid, projection status `PRIOR_ONLY`, zero iterations/KL and confidence D with
`NO_MARKET_CONSTRAINTS`. Tests compare the entire returned Stage-8 JSON exactly
against the accepted service for both horizons. No scoring, probability,
confidence or optimization kernel is duplicated or modified.

The accepted historical OpenFootball prior uses three pinned complete seasons
at commit `f27dcbef681db2c3195f9def62316ce497278781`, with the existing league
home/away means `1.613158`/`1.374561`. Those are historical league rates, not
current opponent-strength evidence. This candidate's executable tests use owned
inputs and claim no real projection. Team strength remains shadow-only.

The existing private current root contract requires Odds input, a market
identity bridge and exact root market constraints. Existing future fallback
requires unavailable-market evidence from the genuine complete Odds response.
Those validators are preserved. A no-Odds private workflow would need a new
separately accepted composition contract, current official fixture identities,
source/purpose approval and full Stage-7/9/optimizer integration. Creating fake
market evidence to pass the old contract is forbidden.

Numerical parity here means parity with the accepted **empty-constraint**
Stage-8 service. Market-primary decision parity is not expected after removing
information and is not claimed. No Stage-9, full recommendation or prospective
accuracy claim is made by this bounded module. A later application candidate
must retain the existing optimizer and report its changes against market-primary
inputs. League priors, stale Grade-E allocation and minutes assumptions can be
materially weak; no historical synthetic test proves their predictive quality.
