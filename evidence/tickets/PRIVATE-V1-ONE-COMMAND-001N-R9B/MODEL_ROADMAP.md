# R9B diagnostic-led research priorities (not activation)

Evidence is synthetic, deliberately fallback-heavy, and not a population estimate.
The order below prioritizes what was measurable and what would unblock a truthful
decision. It is not a claim of expected real-world accuracy improvement.

1. **R9C governed live shadow observation and predeclared sensitivity review.**
   Technical aggregate probe exists. Verify the real current catalogue and its
   fallback split without persistence. Synthetic stale-to-central team assist TV
   median is 0.44374; low-to-high median is 0.11208 (max 0.11558). Those large
   movements require human criteria, not choosing a world on its updating data.
2. **Assist-definition bridge, shrinkage and fallback validation.** Assist-only
   mean absolute player xP movement is 0.32156, the largest tested channel, versus
   0.15057 discipline and 0.01420 GK saves. Historical broad assists versus FPL
   assists are not proven equivalent. Whole-catalogue central assist-weight absolute
   median is 0.00849, p90 0.09634. Holdout evaluation and lawful retention need
   their own authority; no hyperparameter fit is licensed or performed here.
3. **Discipline exposure semantics and sensitivity assessment.** In the frozen
   raw 15-player subset, discipline-only mean delta is -1.796875, compared with
   +0.0625 assist-only and +0.167969 save-only. This is the largest subset mean
   effect, although ablation deltas need not add due to simulator interactions.
   Zero-minute cards are excluded and counted, not divided by zero or imputed.
4. **Non-penalty goal decomposition.** Total-goal rate diagnostic absolute median
   movement is 0.01013, p90 0.27757, but goal-share movement is exactly zero by
   construction. This identifies a potentially material blocked channel, NOT a
   measured goal xP benefit. Direct decomposed evidence and separate governance
   are prerequisites; a donor penalty fraction is not observed decomposition.
5. **Direct defensive definitions and prior strengths, then BPS auxiliaries.**
   Combined CBI/tackles/recoveries have position-level coverage, observed exposure,
   per90 and variance, plus inherited count expectations. These diagnose gaps;
   source-definition equivalence is unproven. Aggregate BPS/bonus cannot identify
   key passes, fouls, passes or dribbles. Do not split CBI or infer residual rates.

GK saves have smaller xP effects in this specific fixture, not proven universal
unimportance. Central absolute max movement is 0.65249 saves/90 and positive-prior
relative max 0.21296. Structural non-GK zeroes explain an all-catalogue zero median.
Low exposure does not mechanically dominate this constructed sample: 0-89-minute
assist median movement is zero, 180-269 is 0.05202, and 360+ is 0.03554. Different
likelihoods and 658 fallback assignments confound population inference. Matched
metamorphic tests, not these unmatched strata, establish exposure/event monotonicity.

Tactical roles, xG/xA, penalty conversion and future injury transitions were not
measured here and cannot honestly be numerically ranked against tested channels.
