   existing FPL private authority. Confirm that fixed conjugate posterior inference

   unknown capability, stop live integration. No inferred legal permission.

   donors; publish only aggregate counts. The synthetic 659-player proof is NOT
   the 659-player live count. Reconcile every comparable season-total exposure;
   disclose partial-row reasons individually in memory, aggregated in output.
3. No live rows, posterior tables, caches, backup, models or training artifacts are
   persisted. Existing raw/derived storage and training DENY remain unchanged.
4. Review central/low/high movement and team TV with predeclared human materiality
   criteria; do not select the best world against its updating observations.
5. Assess Stage-9 paired movement, stochastic error and component interactions.
   Hold Stage-7/8 and seed/scenario identities fixed. Named RNG streams couple the
   experiments but do not remove finite Monte Carlo uncertainty or conditional
   branch draw-consumption differences. Never claim noise-free analytic effects.
6. Complete the **three-GW decision adapter** before claiming horizon effects:
   accept a sealed synthetic R8B rolling execution; compile one cutoff-bound shadow;
   inject supported profiles into each fixture in a test-only request seam; retain
   canonical prior identity as separate shadow provenance, never stale-source
   provenance; reassemble all three sealed scenario projections with existing
   Stage-9/R7 contracts; hold prices, state/history, budgets, candidate scope,
   action scopes and all scenario indexes fixed. Compare main frontier AND
   no-transfer baseline. The R9B provided-squad Stage-10 experiment does not include
   transfers/hits/FT recourse or prove that rolling transfer choices change.
7. Independent model/engineering review, then an explicit human activation decision.
   Choose supported fields and temporary world deliberately. New PlayerPriorIdentity

## Evaluation design (not fitted or run on live observations)

Existing `evaluation/calibration.py::fit_calibration_artifact` fits logistic or
isotonic calibrators, rejects outer-fold contamination, and imposes a training
cutoff. `evaluation/artifacts.py::persist_artifact` writes sealed retained
artifacts. Neither is an appropriate automatic live R9B integration under DENY
training/derived-storage rights. They are deliberately not called.

A later authorized evaluation should freeze each origin's prior/likelihood cutoff,
hyperparameters and fallback map before its holdout Gameweek. Never use the same
GW1-4 observations for updating and reported predictive validation. Fixed-world
comparisons may report held-out count log score, occurrence Brier score and
Poisson deviance with explicit zero-mean conventions; posterior predictive
intervals must use Gamma-Poisson (negative-binomial) predictive uncertainty, not
plug-in Poisson intervals labelled Bayesian. Stratify coverage by position,
fallback assignment and exposure. Compare assist-definition bridge separately.
No future calibration or retention capability is granted by this design.
