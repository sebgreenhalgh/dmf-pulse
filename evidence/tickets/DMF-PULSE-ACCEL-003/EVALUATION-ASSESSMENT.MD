## Actuals compatibility proposal

Current `actuals.py` is labelled a candidate schema and requires exactly13 explicit explain stats per fixture, no extra fields, max3 fixture explain entries, a specific GW-wide live path and an exact2000-player bound. Public textual documentation establishes scoring meanings and finality but does not establish that live JSON carries all13 zero-valued fields, `defensive_contribution` spelling, start status, stats additions, maximum fixture count, fixture assignment after transfer or exact zero/blank encoding. Therefore v1 cannot be called live-schema verified. Keep its strictness and owned fixtures unchanged; publish a v2 compatibility proposal that names these unknowns. A future authorized schema observation must bind exact season/routes/account/body hash/retrieval time and never silently turn sparse fields into zero.

Official current2026/27 text supports09:00 Europe/London the day after the final GW match, real-time provisional bonus, post-match Opta reviews and rare extraordinary corrections. The older PL FAQ/news article4661030 still describes daily one-hour bonus/finality and is stale relative to current help/new. Do not regress to that earlier policy. Require explicit finality evidence and all fixtures finished in addition to the computed09:00 lock; after-lock time alone is not proof. DST boundaries must use London calendar dates. Append-only extraordinary corrections retain original score/report and select one explicit complete vintage; no mixed latest labels.

Official scoring explanation confirms minutes exclude stoppage time; exact60 threshold, per-fixture defensive-contribution cap, goals/assists/CS/save/penalty/card/own-goal/bonus point components. FPL-specific assists/bonus cannot be inferred from generic football goals and assists. Canonical player identity must remain season scoped; club/position should be frozen as-of forecast origin and per-fixture mappings rather than remapped from current club after a transfer. Missing player, explain entries or component is a blocked cohort, not attrition dropped from metrics.

Adversarial compatibility acceptance: sparse/extra/duplicate fields; bool-as-int; invalid totals/counts; wrong season/GW/player/fixture; duplicate identity; blank and double GW; transferred-player continuity; naive time; DST; before-lock and interim bonus; after-lock-but-unchecked event; missing start label; correction forks/mixed vintages; bad original source bytes; forecast receipt timestamp or model substitution; holdout/model/source population mismatch. Every denied retention case must prove zero write/connection/transport calls.
