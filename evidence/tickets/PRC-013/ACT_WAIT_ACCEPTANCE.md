# ACT/WAIT acceptance

`ACT_NOW`, `WAIT_FOR_INFORMATION` and `DO_NOT_TRANSFER` are mandatory. Ranking uses complete utility:
football value, affordability, price scenarios, outgoing selling risk, free transfers, recourse,
information, hits, injury/rotation, reversal, lost purchase position and execution risk. Price
probability is context only and never a direct threshold.

The decision boundary verifies the sealed projection, exact dataset-mode lineage and active policy
status. The decision artifact itself enforces the complete-utility maximum when actionable and
manual-review fail-closed behavior otherwise. Adversarial coverage includes a mode-bypass attempt,
tampered projection, price-only hit, false alarm, information value and expiring free transfer.
