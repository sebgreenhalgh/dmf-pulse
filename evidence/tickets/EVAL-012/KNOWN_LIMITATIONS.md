# Known limitations

1. Acceptance is synthetic and labelled `COUNTERFACTUAL`/`SYNTHETIC`; it is not a historical
   forecast or policy superiority claim.
2. No lawful, complete strict-live historical corpus was present. No history was downloaded or
   represented as `LIVE_OBSERVED`.
3. B4 is frozen to capabilities genuinely accepted at parent
   `4f1274ccef419a7c0bde335c48bd4070e248b2e6`; Stage 13+ models are excluded.
4. B5 variants are explicitly infeasible, hindsight-only, unattainable oracles and cannot become
   production recommendations.
5. Stage 12 changes no relational persistence contract, so it introduces no migration. The
   inherited Alembic head was applied only to exercise the repository PostgreSQL suite.
6. Repository-wide coverage was not run because the review brief requires focused new-code
   coverage and explicitly discourages an expensive repository-wide coverage run.
7. Human acceptance, merge and an accepted tag remain pending and outside reviewer authority.
