# Known limitations

- P0 does not fit, evaluate prospectively, or activate the selected statistical model.
- The empirical values are reconstructed out-of-time shadow evidence, not proof of historical
  operational availability and not a production-superiority claim.
- Parameter uncertainty is not propagated; `CURRENT-TEAM-STRENGTH-001U` is required before any
  production promotion.
- Passing the 10-Gameweek and 100-labelled-fixture gates does not itself authorize promotion.
- Current FPL team IDs are external `2026/27` identifiers only and are not canonical identity.
- Freshness helpers classify already-computed validation signals; acquisition and full fixture
  finality processing belong to downstream implementation.
- The identity registry covers the accepted 2010/11–2026/27 English Premier League corpus only;
  it grants no inferred authority for other competitions or provider purposes.
- Human acceptance, merge, and accepted tagging remain separate and were not performed.
