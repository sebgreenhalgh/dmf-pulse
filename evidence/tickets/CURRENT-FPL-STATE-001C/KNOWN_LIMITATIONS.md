# CURRENT-FPL-STATE-001C known limitations

- Manager facts are human-attested operator declarations, not authenticated or provider-verified
  FPL manager state. The bundle says this structurally.
- There is no automated FPL manager acquisition, authentication, browser automation, manager
  endpoint, account identifier, password, cookie, session, or credential handling.
- The declaration and validated result are private transient in-memory values. There is no raw or
  derived storage, cache, backup, database, registry, replay log, or repository capture.
- Overall points and overall rank may be absent and, when present, remain operator-declared and
  not provider-verified.
- Active or pending Free Hit blocks because 001C does not have sufficient restoration-bearing
  permanent squad/bank/purchase-price state to distinguish temporary effective ownership from
  post-Gameweek restoration state.
- A declared club-quota breach is rejected. No externally created/grandfathered violation state is
  inferred without an explicit accepted contract.
- 001C represents truthful current facts only. It does not fabricate Stage-11 ownership start
  Gameweeks, ownership spells, predecessor nodes, transitions, or historical realised sales.
- The product requires an externally supplied integrity-valid ACTIVE target ruleset plus its exact
  FULL_SEASON capability. Tests create such an ACTIVE input in memory from the accepted verified
  rules artifact and recompute its self-hash; they do not publish an active registry or activate
  production rules.
- 001C is not the unified CURRENT-FPL-STATE-001D composition and performs no availability,
  minutes, projection, optimisation, chip optimisation, or orchestration work.
- Independent review, human acceptance, PR creation, merge, and production activation remain
separate and are not claimed.
